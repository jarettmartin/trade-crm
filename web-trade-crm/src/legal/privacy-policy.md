# Sprout — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 12, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Sprout is a customer, job, and invoicing application operated by Jarett Martin as an individual from Ontario, Canada.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Sprout collects, how that information is used, how it is stored, and how you can contact the operator about your information.

Sprout is currently a free, invite-only beta. Users from across Canada and the United States may participate, subject to eligibility and applicable law.

## 2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Sprout, we may collect the following categories of information:

**Account information**
- Name and email address.
- Account identifiers and authentication-related information needed to manage your account.

**Business information you enter**
- Customer names, contact information, and addresses.
- Job details, notes, service items, prices, and related records.
- Invoice details, line items, tax settings, and generated invoice information.
- Business and catalog information you choose to store in the application.

**Support and communications**
- Information you provide when contacting the operator, requesting account deletion, or submitting feedback.

**Technical information**
- Information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service, such as technical logs or error information where generated by the application or its infrastructure.

Sprout does not currently intentionally use third-party analytics tools to track user behaviour. If analytics or additional observability tools are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated as appropriate before or when those practices begin.

## 3. How we use information

Information may be used to:

- Create and administer accounts and authenticate users.
- Provide customer, job, catalog, and invoicing functionality.
- Store and display records you enter.
- Generate invoices and other requested application outputs.
- Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and secure Sprout.
- Respond to support inquiries and process account deletion requests.
- Diagnose errors and improve reliability.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Business customer information

You are responsible for the information you enter about your own customers, employees, or other individuals. Before entering that information, you must have the rights and legal authority necessary to do so.

Use Sprout only for information appropriate to its intended business-management functions. Do not enter payment-card numbers, bank credentials, government identification numbers, or sensitive health information.

You remain responsible for maintaining your own business records and ensuring that your use of personal information complies with applicable privacy laws.

## 5. Service providers and processing locations

Sprout uses Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure and related AWS services to operate the application. Depending on the service and configuration, AWS may support hosting, data storage, authentication, email delivery, and related operational functions.

Information may be processed or stored in the AWS regions configured for the service. Processing locations depend on the specific services and deployment configuration. Information may be processed outside your province, state, or country, subject to the services used and applicable law.

Third-party providers may process information on the operator's behalf to deliver their services. The operator remains responsible for handling information under their control in accordance with applicable legal obligations.

Sprout does not currently use third-party analytics tools. If additional monitoring, observability, or analytics services are introduced, they may process technical or usage information as necessary for their stated purposes, and this policy will be updated as appropriate.

## 6. Data storage, backups, and retention

Sprout stores information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, support its operation, comply with applicable legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

**Backups:** Sprout performs nightly data backups and retains those backups for seven days. Backups are intended to support recovery but are not a guarantee against data loss. In a catastrophic failure, changes made since the latest usable backup may not be recoverable, potentially resulting in the loss of approximately one day's changes.

**Account deletion:** Account deletion is handled manually. After a deletion request is processed, information will be removed from active systems as reasonably practicable. Copies may remain in backups until those backups expire under the seven-day retention schedule. Information may be retained longer where reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or other lawful purposes.

You should keep independent copies of important business records and should not rely on Sprout during the beta as the only repository for essential records.

## 7. Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to help protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse. No online service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

The beta may contain defects or experience service interruptions and data loss. Users should use appropriate account security practices and avoid storing information in Sprout that is unnecessary or excessively sensitive.

If a security incident occurs, the operator will respond as appropriate and comply with applicable legal obligations.
